Little Ages / Style board, round 2

Style board

Redrawn after your notes. The terrain is now chunky tiles like WorldBox, with banded water and land that casts a shadow. People bob, lean and swing with weight, every job kicks up chips, dust or splashes, and two new interface ideas sit on top of the map. Everything is still drawn live in your browser. Pick again at the bottom.

A living world

Three young kingdoms on one continent. Zoom from the whole world down to a single village: at the world view people are specks with kingdom borders, and up close they switch to the detailed 8 by 16 sprites. Drag to look around.

People

Genetics in one family

A dark-skinned mother with tight curls and a light-skinned father with straight brown hair. Their children land in between, and each one comes out a little different.

Two sizes

The same five people at world, region and close-up sizes. The game switches between them as you zoom.

Actions

Each work animation has a wind-up, an impact frame held a beat longer, and a recovery, with particles on the hit. Walks bob on the step and the arms swing against the legs.

Homes and cultures

Each culture builds in its own style, and each kingdom paints its roofs in its own color so borders read at a glance. Wealth changes size, decoration and upkeep.

Seasons

One stretch of riverside through a year, using the same terrain renderer as the world map.

Underground

Cross-section view, redrawn in the new palette. A miner climbs the shaft while another works the copper vein, and groundwater collects in the cave below.

Two interface ideas

Pinned field tags: click anyone to pin a paper tag to them. A thread follows them as they walk, and relationship threads show family, friends and feuds. History ribbon: a timeline along the bottom with a little banner for every big moment. Click a banner to fly to where it happened, or drag along the ribbon to look back in time.
