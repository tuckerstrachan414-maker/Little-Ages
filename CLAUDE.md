# Working notes

- When a pull request is wanted, open a normal one, not a draft.
- The owner may ask for changes to be pushed straight to `main`; do that when they say so.
- Hard, messy outcomes that come out of what people decide are realism, not bugs. A band shivering by a dead fire because its only fire-maker is out, or hungry families moving back and forth between bands, make the game feel real. Don't add rules to smooth them away; if something looks wrong, ask the owner first.
